How staff salary is calculated from attendance

  1. Per-day salary = monthly salary ÷ the days you divide by (the days in the month, 30 or 26).
  2. Payable days = days present + paid leave + paid weekly offs + (half-days × 0.5).
  3. Salary for days worked = per-day salary × payable days.
  4. Overtime pay = per-day salary ÷ normal hours per day × overtime hours × overtime rate.
  5. Amount to pay = salary for days worked + overtime pay − advance or deductions.

Example: ₹15,000 a month in a 30-day month is ₹500 a day. With 24 days present, 1 half-day and 4 paid weekly offs, payable days are 28.5 and the salary is ₹14,250. Six hours of overtime at 2× an hourly rate of ₹62.50 adds ₹750. Less a ₹1,000 advance, the amount to pay is ₹14,000.

Overtime calculator: the rules in 2026

The four labour codes were notified on 21 November 2025, and the central rules followed in 2026. Under them, overtime is paid at twice the normal rate of wages, and the normal limit is 8 hours a day and 48 hours a week under the OSH Code, with a cap of 144 overtime hours in a quarter. State rules for shops and establishments can differ in detail, so treat this calculator as a guide and confirm with your CA.

Frequently asked questions

How do I calculate salary from attendance?

Per-day salary = monthly salary ÷ days in the month. Payable days = present + paid leave + paid weekly offs + half-days × 0.5. Salary = per-day salary × payable days, plus overtime, minus any advance.

Should I divide monthly salary by 30, 26 or the days in the month?

Businesses use all three. Dividing by the calendar days (28 to 31) is the most common when weekly offs are paid. Dividing by 26 is used when only working days are paid. Pick one rule, tell your staff, and use it every month.

How is overtime calculated?

Hourly rate = per-day salary ÷ normal working hours in a day. Overtime pay = hourly rate × overtime hours × the overtime rate. Under the new labour codes, overtime is paid at twice the normal rate of wages.

What is the overtime rate in India in 2026?

Twice the normal rate of wages under the labour codes, for work beyond the daily or weekly limit (8 hours a day and 48 hours a week under the OSH Code). State rules and your category of establishment can change the details, so confirm with your CA.

Can this calculator handle advances given during the month?

Yes. Enter the advance or other deduction, and it is taken off the salary to give the amount to pay.
