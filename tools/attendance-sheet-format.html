Monthly attendance sheet format

Whether you call it an attendance register, a staff attendance sheet or an employee attendance sheet, a monthly format has the same parts:

  1. Business name and month at the top
  2. One row per employee with name and role
  3. A column for each day of the month (1 to 31)
  4. A code in each cell: P, A, HD, L or WO
  5. Totals at the end of each row, and payable days for salary

Salary sheet format from attendance

The second sheet in the download is a salary sheet: monthly salary, payable days, per-day rate, overtime and advances, and the net amount for each employee. The formula it uses:

Salary = monthly salary ÷ days in the month × payable days. Some businesses divide by 30 or 26 instead of the calendar days; pick one rule and use it every month. To try different rules, use the free salary and overtime calculator.

Frequently asked questions

What should a staff attendance sheet include?

The month, each employee's name and role, one column for every day of the month, a code for each day (P, A, HD, L, WO), and totals for present days, absences, half-days, leave and week offs. Add payable days and salary if you use it for payroll.

What codes are used in an attendance register?

P for present, A for absent, HD for half-day, L for paid leave and WO for weekly off. Some businesses add H for a holiday. The template counts each code automatically.

How do I calculate payable days from an attendance sheet?

Payable days = present days + paid leave + paid weekly offs + (half-days × 0.5). Absent days are not paid. Salary for the month = monthly salary ÷ days in the month × payable days.

How do I calculate attendance percentage?

Attendance % = days present ÷ working days × 100. Count a half-day as 0.5. For example, 23.5 days present out of 26 working days is 90.4%.
