How to calculate GST

Adding GST to a price

GST amount = Price × GST rate ÷ 100, and Total = Price + GST.

Example: an item priced at ₹1,000 with 18% GST → GST is ₹1,000 × 18 ÷ 100 = ₹180, so the customer pays ₹1,180.

Removing GST from a price that already includes it

Price before GST = Inclusive price ÷ (1 + GST rate ÷ 100). For 18%, divide by 1.18; for 5%, divide by 1.05.

Example: ₹1,180 including 18% GST → ₹1,180 ÷ 1.18 = ₹1,000 before GST, and the GST inside it is ₹180. A common mistake is to take 18% of ₹1,180 (₹212.40) — that is wrong.

CGST, SGST and IGST — which one applies?

  • Customer in the same state as you: the GST is split into two equal halves — CGST (Central) and SGST (State). 18% = 9% CGST + 9% SGST; 5% = 2.5% CGST + 2.5% SGST. On ₹1,000 at 18% that is ₹90 + ₹90.
  • Customer in another state: the full rate is charged as IGST. On ₹1,000 at 18% that is ₹180 IGST.

The total tax is the same either way — only how it is shown on the bill changes.

Current GST rates (GST 2.0)

Since 22 September 2025, GST has four main slabs. The old 12% and 28% slabs were removed; most items in them moved to 5% or 18%.

RateTypically covers
0% (exempt)Many basic essentials, such as most unpacked fresh food
5%Many everyday goods, and most restaurant food service
18%The standard rate — most goods and services
40%Luxury and "sin" goods

A few special rates also exist (for example 3% on gold and 0.25% on rough diamonds). The rate for your item depends on its HSN/SAC code — always check the current rate on the official GST rates page.

Frequently asked questions

How do I calculate 18% GST on ₹1,000?

18% of ₹1,000 is ₹180, so the total is ₹1,180. Within the same state that is ₹90 CGST + ₹90 SGST; for a customer in another state it is ₹180 IGST.

How do I remove GST from a price that includes it?

Divide the price by (1 + rate/100). For 18% GST divide by 1.18: ₹1,180 ÷ 1.18 = ₹1,000, so the GST inside is ₹180.

Is 12% or 28% GST still charged?

No. From 22 September 2025 (GST 2.0) the 12% and 28% slabs were removed. Most items moved to 5% or 18%, and some luxury and sin goods to 40%. Check the current rate for your HSN code on the GST portal.

What is the GST on restaurant food?

Most restaurants charge 5% GST (2.5% CGST + 2.5% SGST) without input tax credit. Restaurants inside hotels with room tariffs above ₹7,500 per night charge 18%. Check the current rule on the GST portal.
