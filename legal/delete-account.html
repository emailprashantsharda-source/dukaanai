Delete your account

Last updated: 17 April 2026

You can permanently delete your HisaabNow account and all associated data at any time. This page explains the two ways to do it and what happens to your data afterwards.

⚠️ Important: Account deletion is permanent. All your shops, products, bills, customers, inventory, expenses, and subscription history will be erased and cannot be recovered. Make sure you've exported any data you want to keep before deleting.

Option 1 — Delete from inside the app (recommended)

Fastest · 30 seconds

Steps

  1. Open the HisaabNow app on your phone
  2. Tap Settings (gear icon)
  3. Scroll down and tap Delete My Account
  4. Read the confirmation message
  5. Type the word DELETE to confirm
  6. Tap Delete Permanently

Your account is queued for deletion. You will be logged out immediately, and your data will be removed within 24 hours.

Option 2 — Email request

If you can't access the app

What gets deleted

  • Your name, phone number, and shop details
  • All products, prices, and inventory records
  • All sales, bills, and customer records
  • All expense and supplier records
  • Staff and commission settings
  • AI insight history and chat logs with Munim
  • App preferences and configuration
  • Push notification tokens

What we are required to keep

Indian tax law requires us to retain certain financial records for 7 years (GST compliance). After your account is deleted, we keep only the following:

Data type Retention period Why
Payment invoices (subscription) 7 years Indian tax law (Section 36 of CGST Act)
Anonymized usage stats Indefinite Aggregated, cannot be linked to you
Crash logs 90 days To fix bugs that affect other users

This retained data does not include your shop's transaction records, customers, or personal information. It cannot be used to identify you after deletion.

Timeline

  • Immediately: You are logged out, your account is marked for deletion, and no new logins are accepted
  • Within 24 hours: Personal data is removed from active servers
  • Within 30 days: Personal data is also removed from backups
  • Within 90 days: All non-essential traces (crash logs, sync queues) are purged

What happens to my subscription?

If you have an active paid subscription:

  • Monthly plans: Cancelled immediately. No refund for the partial month.
  • Annual plans: If you have used the app less than 10 times in the past 14 days, a pro-rated refund is available. Otherwise, the subscription is cancelled and no refund is given.
  • Google Play subscriptions: You must also cancel via Google Play Store (Account → Subscriptions) to stop future charges.

Can I reactivate my account later?

No. Deletion is permanent. If you want to use HisaabNow again, you will need to sign up as a new user and rebuild your data from scratch.

If you're having a problem we can solve

If you're deleting because of a bug, billing issue, or unhappiness with a feature, please reach out first. We might be able to fix it. Most issues are resolved in under 24 hours.
