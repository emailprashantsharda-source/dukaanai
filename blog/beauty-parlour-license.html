Beauty Parlour License in India: What You Need Before You Open

By HisaabNow Team · 12 November 2026 · 6 min read

There is no single "beauty parlour license" in India. A parlour usually needs a trade licence from the local municipality, a Shops and Establishments registration from the state, and GST registration once turnover crosses the limit. Which ones apply to you depends on your state and city.

Quick answer: start with the local municipal office for the trade licence and the state labour portal for Shops and Establishments registration. Add GST registration if yearly turnover goes above ₹20 lakh (₹10 lakh in special category states). Fire safety clearance, a labour licence and other items depend on your premises size, staff count and state. Updated 12 November 2026.

Documents you will usually need

  • Your Aadhaar and PAN as identity proof.
  • Proof of the premises: a rent agreement or ownership deed, with a no-objection letter from the landlord if you rent.
  • Passport-size photographs.
  • GST certificate, if you are registered.
  • A health or sanitation certificate and fire clearance, where your city asks for them.

How to apply: the usual steps

  1. Find your portal. Ask the municipal office or search your state's labour department site. The trade licence usually comes from the municipality, and the Shops and Establishments certificate from the state.
  2. Upload the documents in the size and format the portal asks for.
  3. Pay the fee and save the receipt and application number.
  4. Get the premises inspected. One guide says municipal inspections usually happen within 7 to 15 days and look at cleanliness, waste disposal, sterilisation of tools, ventilation and lighting.
  5. Download the certificate and display it at the parlour.

Fees and timelines vary widely. One guide puts trade licence fees at ₹500 to ₹5,000, usually lower in smaller cities, with approval taking from about a week to a month. Treat that as a rough guide and ask your municipality for the actual fee. Each branch needs its own licence.

GST: do you need to register?

A service provider must register for GST when yearly turnover goes above ₹20 lakh (₹10 lakh in special category states). Salon and beauty services are taxed at 5% without input tax credit from 22 September 2025. If you are registered, put your GSTIN and the tax split on every bill: see our salon bill format with a free Excel template. If you are not registered, you cannot charge GST, so issue a plain bill.

Mistakes to avoid

  • Signing the rent agreement before checking the rules. Some licences need premises of a certain type or size.
  • Relying on an agent's word. Ask for the fee receipt and the certificate in your own name.
  • Forgetting renewals. Put the renewal dates in your calendar.
  • Mixing up GST and the licences. GST registration does not replace a trade licence.

Frequently asked questions

Which license is needed to open a beauty parlour in India?

Usually a trade licence from the local municipality and a Shops and Establishments registration from the state. GST registration is needed once turnover crosses the limit. Other items depend on your city and premises.

Is GST registration compulsory for a beauty parlour?

Only when yearly turnover goes above ₹20 lakh (₹10 lakh in special category states). Below that, registration is optional.

How long does it take to get a trade licence for a salon?

It varies by city. One guide says about 7 to 15 days in smaller cities and 2 to 4 weeks in big cities once documents are in order. Ask your municipal office for the current time.

Do I need a fire NOC for a small parlour?

It depends on your city and the size of the premises. One guide says it applies to salons above about 500 sq ft. Check with your local fire department.

Do I need a separate licence for each branch?

Yes. According to one guide, each branch needs its own trade licence and Shops and Establishments registration.
