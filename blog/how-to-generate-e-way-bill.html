How to Generate an E Way Bill in India

By HisaabNow Team · 3 October 2026 · 8 min read

Discover the steps to generate an e-way bill in India, ensuring smooth transport of goods across states and compliance with GST regulations.

Understanding E Way Bills

An e-way bill is a mandatory document required for the movement of goods worth more than ₹50,000 within India (check the current rule on the GST portal). It is part of the GST regime and helps streamline the transportation of goods by ensuring compliance and reducing tax evasion. Think of it as a digital pass that ensures your goods are accounted for during transit.

Shop owners need to generate an e-way bill when transporting goods inter-state if the consignment value exceeds the threshold of ₹50,000 (check the current rule on the GST portal). For example, if you are sending a bulk order of groceries worth ₹60,000 from Delhi to Mumbai, an e-way bill is required. However, there are exceptions like transporting goods for personal use or exempted goods, where an e-way bill might not be necessary.

The key components of an e-way bill include:

  • Part A: Details of the consignor, consignee, and the goods being transported. This includes the GSTIN of the supplier and recipient, place of delivery, invoice or challan number, and the value of goods.
  • Part B: Transport details, such as the vehicle number, which is crucial for the movement of goods.

An e-way bill is generated against a tax invoice or delivery challan, so the invoice or challan must be correct first. Understanding these aspects of an e-way bill ensures smooth transactions and compliance with GST regulations, helping avoid potential penalties.

Registering on the E Way Bill Portal

Registering on the E Way Bill portal is a straightforward process, but it requires some essential documents. First, visit the official E Way Bill portal and click on the 'Registration' option. If you are a registered GST taxpayer, select 'e-Way Bill Registration'. Enter your GSTIN and the captcha code, then click 'Go'. The system will fetch your details from the GST portal.

You will need certain documents for registration, including your GSTIN, business name, address, and contact details. Ensure that these details match those on the GST portal to avoid any discrepancies.

Once registered, logging in is simple for registered users. Go back to the E Way Bill portal, click on 'Login', and enter your username and password. If you forget your password, there is an option to reset it using your registered email or mobile number.

Steps to Generate an E Way Bill

Generating an E Way Bill is a straightforward process once you get familiar with the steps. First, log in to the E Way Bill portal using your credentials. Once logged in, select the 'Generate New' option from the menu.

Next, you will see a form with several fields that need to be filled. Start with the Transaction Type; choose whether it’s an outward or inward supply. Then, enter the Sub-Type, such as supply, export, import, etc. Enter the Document Type and Document Number (like an invoice number), along with the Document Date.

Fill in the From/To section with the details of the supplier and recipient, including their GSTIN, if applicable. Enter the Item Details, including the HSN code, product description, quantity, and value. Make sure to select the correct GST Rate (e.g., 5%, 18%, or 40%).

Next, provide the Transporter Details such as the vehicle number and transporter ID. Once all fields are completed, click 'Submit' to generate the E Way Bill. You will receive an E Way Bill number which you can print or save.

Common mistakes to avoid include incorrect GSTIN entries, wrong document dates, and mismatched HSN codes. Double-check these details to prevent errors. Remember, accurate information ensures compliance and avoids penalties.

Handling E Way Bill Amendments and Cancellations

Handling amendments and cancellations of e-way bills is crucial for maintaining compliance and avoiding penalties. Here’s a simple guide on how to manage these changes effectively.

Amending an E Way Bill

If you need to amend an e-way bill, you can do so on the e-way bill portal. Amendments are allowed only for certain fields such as the vehicle number, transporter details, or the date of transportation. For example, if you initially entered the wrong vehicle number, you can update it once the goods are in transit. However, core details like the GSTIN of the supplier or recipient, the value of goods, and the HSN code cannot be amended. In such cases, you must cancel the existing bill and generate a new one.

Cancelling an E Way Bill

Cancellation of an e-way bill is necessary if the goods are not transported, or if there are significant changes in the shipment details. You must cancel the bill within 24 hours of its generation. To cancel, log into the e-way bill portal, select the ‘Cancel’ option, and provide a reason for cancellation. Remember, once an e-way bill is verified by a tax officer, it cannot be cancelled.

When to Amend or Cancel

You may need to amend or cancel an e-way bill due to incorrect details entered initially, changes in transportation plans, or if the transaction is called off. Always ensure that the amendments or cancellations are done promptly to avoid any compliance issues.

Compliance and Penalties

Compliance with e-way bill regulations is crucial for any business involved in the transportation of goods. An e-way bill is a mandatory document for moving goods worth more than ₹50,000. It ensures transparency and prevents tax evasion during the transit of goods.

Non-compliance with e-way bill rules can lead to significant penalties. If an e-way bill is not generated, the penalty can be as high as ₹10,000 or the tax amount sought to be evaded, whichever is greater. Additionally, the goods can be detained or seized, causing delays and additional costs.

To ensure compliance and avoid penalties, here are a few tips:

  • Timely Generation: Always generate the e-way bill before the commencement of goods movement. Double-check the details for accuracy.

Frequently asked questions

What is the validity period of an e-way bill?

The validity depends on the distance goods need to travel. Generally, it's one day for every 200 km (20 km per day for over-dimensional cargo).

Can an e-way bill be generated for exempt goods?

No, e-way bills are typically not required for goods that are exempt from GST.

Is it mandatory to carry a physical copy of the e-way bill?

No, a digital copy is sufficient as long as it can be produced on demand during transit.

What should I do if there is a mistake in the e-way bill?

Amendments can be made to correct errors, but ensure that the e-way bill is accurate before generation to avoid complications.
