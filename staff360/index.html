Staff360 is HisaabNow's staff attendance and workforce product. It helps you mark attendance, manage shifts and leave, track hours and late arrivals, and — on King PRO — get AI insights about your team. Use it on its own at hisaabnow.com/staff360, or inside the HisaabNow billing app if you already use it.
