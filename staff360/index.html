How it works

Set up in minutes from the HisaabNow app you already use.

For the owner or manager

  1. Open Staff in HisaabNow and add your team.
  2. Mark today's attendance, or let staff check in themselves (Premium).
  3. Create shifts and grace time so late arrivals are counted.
  4. Approve leave, check reports, and review AI insights (King PRO).

For the employee (Premium & King PRO)

  1. Log in to HisaabNow with their own mobile number.
  2. Tap Check in on arrival — location is captured once.
  3. Tap Check out when leaving. Hours are calculated automatically.
  4. See their own attendance and request leave. They never see anyone else's data.

Questions owners ask

Does Staff360 track my employees all day?

No. Location is read only at the moment an employee taps Check in or Check out. There is no background or continuous tracking.

Does it use face recognition or selfies?

No. Staff360 does not use face recognition, selfies or any biometric data on any plan.

Is GPS proof that someone was in the shop?

GPS is strong evidence, shown with its accuracy in metres, but phones can be imprecise indoors. Staff360 shows you the accuracy so you can judge it.

What if an employee's location is off?

The app explains how to turn it on. You choose whether check-in is blocked or allowed and marked “no GPS”. Staff360 never fills in a location by itself.

Does the AI pay salaries?

No. It prepares a draft estimate with the full calculation — payable days, half-days, leave and overtime. You review and approve it.

Do my staff need a smartphone?

Only for self check-in (Premium and King PRO). On Basic, you or your manager mark attendance for everyone.

What happens if my plan ends?

Your staff list and attendance history are kept. Higher-plan features lock until you renew.
