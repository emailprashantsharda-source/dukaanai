FAQ

Questions owners ask

Does Staff360 track my employees all day?

No. Location is read only at the moment an employee taps Check in or Check out. There is no background or continuous tracking.

Does it use face recognition or selfies?

No. Staff360 does not use face recognition, selfies or any biometric data on any plan.

Is GPS proof that someone was at work?

GPS is strong evidence, and Staff360 shows its accuracy in metres so you can judge it — phones can be less precise indoors.

What if an employee's location is off?

The app explains how to turn it on. You choose whether check-in is blocked or allowed and marked “no GPS”. Staff360 never fills in a location by itself.

Does the AI pay salaries?

No. It prepares a draft estimate showing the full calculation — payable days, half-days, leave and overtime. You review and approve it. Nothing is paid or filed automatically.

Do my staff need a smartphone?

Only for self check-in on Premium and King PRO. On Basic, you or your manager mark attendance for everyone.

What happens if my plan ends?

Your staff list and attendance history are kept. Higher-plan features lock until you renew.
