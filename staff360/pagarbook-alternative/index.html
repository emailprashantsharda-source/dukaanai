Why owners look for a PagarBook alternative

Staff don't want selfie or face attendance

Many shop and salon staff are uncomfortable sending a selfie or scanning their face every morning. Staff360 was built without any biometric features: staff tap check-in, and the server records the time and the location at that moment.

You already run billing on HisaabNow

If HisaabNow is your billing app, Staff360 lives inside it with the same staff list, so there is one app on the counter instead of two.

You want answers, not just reports

On King PRO you can ask “who was late more than three times this month?” and get the names with the dates behind them. Repeated late arrivals and unusual patterns are flagged for you to review.

How to switch in 10 minutes

  1. Log in at hisaabnow.com/staff360/login with your mobile number.
  2. Create your business and add staff with name, phone and monthly salary.
  3. Start marking attendance from the 1st of the month so the month's totals are clean.
  4. Keep your old PagarBook records for past months.

Frequently asked questions

Is Staff360 a good PagarBook alternative?

It is if you want simple staff attendance without selfies or face scans, GPS captured only at check-in, and attendance that sits next to your HisaabNow billing. If you need full payroll with payslips, PF and ESI filing or salary payments from the app, PagarBook covers more of that today.

Does Staff360 use selfie or face recognition attendance?

No. Staff360 never asks for a selfie, face scan or fingerprint. Owners mark attendance, or staff check in with their mobile number and OTP, with location recorded only at the moment they tap.

Can I move my staff list from PagarBook to Staff360?

Yes. Add your staff in Staff360 with their name, phone and monthly salary. It takes a few minutes for a small team. Your past PagarBook records stay in PagarBook.

Does Staff360 calculate salary like PagarBook?

King PRO prepares a draft salary estimate from payable days, half-days, approved leave and overtime, and shows the working. You review and approve it. Staff360 does not pay salaries or generate statutory payslips.

How much does Staff360 cost compared with PagarBook?

Staff360 King PRO is ₹4,999 per year per business. Basic is free for up to 2 staff, and Premium is ₹999 a month for up to 10 staff. PagarBook's prices are on its own website, so check there for the current plans.
