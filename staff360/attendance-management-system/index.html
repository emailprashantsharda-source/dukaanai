Why small businesses move off the paper register

A paper attendance register works until month end. Then someone counts ticks for every employee, argues about a half-day nobody wrote down, and works out salary by hand. Biometric machines fix some of that, but they cost money, break, and many staff are uncomfortable giving fingerprints or face scans.

An attendance management app gives you the same register on your phone, adds up the month for you, and keeps a record of every change, so there is one version of the truth when salary day comes.

Time and attendance software without the hardware

Most time attendance software in India is sold with a fingerprint or face machine at the door. Staff360 is cloud-based time and attendance software that needs no device: staff check in on their own phone, and the time, location and accuracy are recorded on the server, so the phone's clock can't be changed to fake a time.

  • No selfies, face scans or fingerprints. Nothing biometric is collected, ever.
  • GPS only at the moment of check-in. No tracking during the day.
  • Real-time attendance. The owner sees who has checked in as it happens.
  • Nothing to install. A light web app that opens in any phone or computer browser.

Attendance and leave management in one place

Leave is where most registers go wrong. With Staff360, staff send a leave request from their phone, you approve or reject it, and approved leave shows on the register automatically. Weekly offs are set once per shift. At month end you see present days, half-days, leave and week offs side by side.

Frequently asked questions

What is an attendance management system?

It is software that records when each employee is present, absent, on half-day or on leave, and turns that into monthly totals for salary. A good one replaces the paper register, keeps a history of every change and lets staff check in from their own phone.

Do I need a biometric machine for attendance?

No. Staff360 runs on the phones and computers you already have. You can mark attendance yourself, or staff check in with their mobile number and OTP. It never uses fingerprints, face scans or selfies.

Is Staff360 an online attendance system?

Yes. It is cloud based, so the owner, managers and staff see the same records from any phone, tablet or computer browser at hisaabnow.com/staff360/login.

Can it handle shifts, leave and overtime?

Yes, on Premium and King PRO: morning, evening and overnight shifts with a grace period, weekly offs, leave requests with approval, working hours and overtime. On Basic the owner or manager marks daily attendance.

How much does the attendance management system cost?

King PRO, with AI insights and draft salary estimates, is ₹4,999 per year per business. Basic is free for up to 2 staff, and Premium is ₹999 a month for up to 10 staff.

Can staff change their own attendance?

No. Employees see only their own attendance. Only the owner or a manager can change a record, and every change keeps the old value, the new value, who changed it and the reason.
