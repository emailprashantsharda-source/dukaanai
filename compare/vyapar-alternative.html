HisaabNow vs Vyapar: an honest comparison

Vyapar is a popular GST billing and accounting app for Indian small businesses. HisaabNow is a billing and POS app for shops that want to see real profit on every sale and bill by voice in Hindi. Here is how they compare.

Choose Vyapar if…

you want broad billing plus accounting — e-invoicing and e-way bills, Tally import/export, delivery challans, OCR scanning of purchase bills and salesman tracking — across many kinds of businesses.

Choose HisaabNow if…

you run a kirana, sweet shop, café, restaurant or salon and care most about fast counter billing, Hindi voice billing, udhaar with automatic WhatsApp reminders and seeing your real profit every day.

Where Vyapar is stronger

  • Accounting and compliance breadth — e-invoice and e-way bill generation, Tally import/export, delivery challans.
  • More business types — manufacturing, pharmacy, jewellery and apparel, not just counter shops.
  • Languages — the app is available in 9+ Indian languages.

Where HisaabNow is stronger

  • Real profit, not just sales. HisaabNow subtracts the cost of every item and shows what you actually earned today.
  • Hindi voice billing for the rush hour — say the items, they go on the bill.
  • Made for your type of shop — weight-based pricing and expiry alerts for sweet shops, recipe costing and KOTs for restaurants, appointments and stylist commissions for salons.
