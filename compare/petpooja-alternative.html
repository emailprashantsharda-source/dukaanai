HisaabNow vs Petpooja: an honest comparison

Petpooja is one of India's best-known restaurant POS systems. HisaabNow is a simpler billing app built around real profit per dish and Hindi voice billing. Here is where each one fits — including where Petpooja is the better choice.

Choose Petpooja if…

you run a larger or multi-outlet restaurant that needs a deep restaurant stack — kitchen display, captain ordering app, QR ordering, restaurant website and loyalty add-ons, with a dedicated point of contact on higher plans.

Choose HisaabNow if…

you run a single restaurant, dhaba, café or cloud kitchen and want fast billing (including by voice in Hindi), recipe-based cost tracking and the real profit on every dish — with published prices and a 7-day free trial.

Where Petpooja is stronger

  • Depth for big restaurants. Kitchen display systems, a captain (waiter) ordering app, QR ordering and a restaurant website are all available as add-ons.
  • Chains and central kitchens. A central kitchen module and unlimited terminals suit multi-outlet operations.
  • Dedicated help. Higher plans include a dedicated point of contact.

Where HisaabNow is stronger

  • Real profit per dish, every day. Define a recipe once (e.g. 200 g chicken + 80 g cream + masalas) and every plate shows its real margin.
  • Hindi voice billing. "Do butter chicken, ek naan" — the order goes on the bill, hands-free during the rush.
  • Simple, published pricing with a 7-day free trial and no card needed.
  • One app beyond restaurants. The same app runs cafés, salons, sweet shops and kirana stores if you own more than one type of business.
