HisaabNow vs Sapaad: an honest comparison

Sapaad is a cloud restaurant POS and management platform used from food trucks to enterprise chains. HisaabNow is a simpler billing app for Indian restaurants and cafés, built around real profit per dish and Hindi voice billing.

Choose Sapaad if…

you need a full restaurant platform — online ordering, delivery app integrations, self-ordering kiosks, kitchen display, a waiter app and advanced analytics — especially across many locations.

Choose HisaabNow if…

you run a single restaurant, dhaba or café and want quick table-wise billing, KOT printing, Hindi voice billing and the real profit on every dish, with published prices.
