HisaabNow vs myBillBook: an honest comparison

myBillBook is a GST billing and accounting app used by retailers, distributors and wholesalers. HisaabNow is a billing and POS app for counter shops that want real profit per sale and Hindi voice billing. Here is how they compare.

Choose myBillBook if…

you are a retailer, distributor or wholesaler who needs e-invoicing, unlimited e-way bills, multiple godowns, recurring billing or Tally data export.

Choose HisaabNow if…

you run a kirana, sweet shop, café, restaurant or salon and want quick counter billing (by barcode or by voice in Hindi), udhaar with WhatsApp reminders and your real profit every day.
