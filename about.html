What HisaabNow does

HisaabNow combines billing, GST invoicing, inventory tracking, customer credit (udhaar) management, and AI business insights in a single mobile-first app. The product is designed for shopkeepers who currently track their business in paper notebooks, Excel sheets, or basic billing apps that show only revenue and not actual profit.

The distinguishing feature is real profit tracking per sale. Every menu item, product, or service has a recipe or cost structure, so when a bill is generated the app calculates not just the revenue but the actual margin earned. This lets owners see which items make them money and which don't, often surprising them — the bestseller is rarely the biggest profit driver.

The app also includes Hindi voice billing — owners can say "do cappuccino, ek brownie" or "do butter chicken, ek naan" and the items are added to the bill, leaving their hands free during peak rush hours. The voice engine parses Hindi numerals and works in noisy shop environments.

The team and location

HisaabNow is built in Noida, Uttar Pradesh, India, by a team of POS and Indian SMB specialists. The product was launched in April 2026 after extended development informed by interviews with shopkeepers across pan shops, sweet shops, kirana stores, and salons. The product roadmap is driven primarily by what owners actually do in their shops every day, not by feature lists imported from US/EU POS systems.

The mission is to help India's 60+ million micro and small business owners stop running their business in notebooks and start running it on real numbers. Every feature is tested against one question: would a shopkeeper in a 200 sq ft shop use this on a Saturday at 8pm?
